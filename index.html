The trouble with "organic"

Label abuse

Organic stamps get treated as a marketing sticker rather than a verified claim, and shoppers have no easy way to tell the difference.

Broken transparency

Tracking a product by hand across farms, processors, and trucks is slow and full of gaps — and gaps are where fraud lives.

Lost credibility

When certification can't be checked, honest growers pay the price alongside the ones cutting corners.

How the record gets built

  1. 01

    Tag it at the source

    Each batch gets a digital identifier the moment it's harvested — no separate paperwork, no re-entry later.

  2. 02

    Record every handoff

    Farm, processor, distributor, retailer — each transfer is written to a shared ledger no single party can quietly edit.

  3. 03

    Let anyone verify it

    A shopper scans a code. A regulator pulls the full history. Each sees exactly the layer of detail meant for them.

    Who sees what

    Not everyone needs the same depth of detail — and not every detail should be public.

    Origin and certification

    Where a product came from, when it was harvested, and whether its organic certification is current — nothing more, nothing hidden.

    Batch, logistics, and compliance

    Full batch numbers, cold-chain data, delivery windows, and compliance documentation for every shipment received.

    The complete ledger

    Every recorded transaction across the network, timestamped and unaltered — the full chain of custody for audits and recalls.
